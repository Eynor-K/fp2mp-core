\subsection{Adaptive Collaboration Depth}

Unlike fixed-round debate (3 rounds in our baseline), FP2MP exhibits adaptive depth.
In the case study, the system performed 17 dispatch segments over 300 tool calls, with BlocksNet returning six times.
The key episode was the critic injecting \texttt{sq\_model\_support\#fu2} after detecting an uncovered aspect (service-population centrality mismatch).
This demonstrates that the combination of blackboard and critic enables problem-driven computational effort, not a predetermined interaction pattern.
For simpler queries, the same loop would terminate earlier.
This property is not present in voting or actor-critic baselines.
